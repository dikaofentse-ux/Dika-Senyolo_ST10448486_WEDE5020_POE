WHO WE ARE

Green-Harvest Organic Farm

Green-Harvest Organic Farm is a South African company founded in 2018.

We offer organic vegetables, fruits, herbs and free-range eggs to homes, restaurants, cafés and supermarkets.

Our goal is to make fresh and healthy food accessible to more South Africans.

🌱

Our Mission

To offer healthy, fresh and affordable organic food and promote sustainable agriculture.

🌍

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's top organic produce suppliers through technology.

❤️

Our Values

Quality, sustainability, affordability, freshness and customer satisfaction.
